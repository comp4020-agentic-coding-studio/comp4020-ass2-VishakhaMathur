# The printed floor plan lies by omission.

---

## It shows where. Not how far.

- milk sits at the back of nearly every supermarket you've entered
- the printed plan shows where things are, never the path forced on you to
  reach them
- that gap is not an oversight, it's the layout's whole argument

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Space is a practiced place.
>
> — Michel de Certeau

---

## The strategy and the tactic, applied

- **strategy** — the aisle order, designed from above, indifferent to any
  one shopper
- **tactic** — the habitual route your body improvises through it anyway
- the gap between the two is the whole discipline of retail geography

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## What the detour costs

Every metre between the door and what you actually needed is a metre spent
on someone else's interest, not yours.
