# You comply with the instruction before you register it as one.

---

## Tempo as instruction, not mood

- ambient music sets a walking pace the way a metronome sets a tempo
- slower near high-margin fresh goods, quicker near the checkout
- the queue feels shorter than it is, on purpose

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## The sonic floor

An ambient instruction, played into every zone of the building, that you
obey before you've noticed it's an instruction at all.

---

## Before next week

- notice the music on your next visit, deliberately, for once
- note where in the store it changed
- decide what a store with no ambient sound at all would do instead
