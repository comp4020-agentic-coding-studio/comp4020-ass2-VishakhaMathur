# Everything up to here was routed for you.

---

## Where the routing runs out

- the queue is the one part of the building nobody designed
- what fills the gap — a magazine rack, a card reader, a stranger who talks
  to you — is the one thing in the store that wasn't planned
- notice it before the machine at the end of it takes even this away

## The queue against the checkout

Everything the queue leaves unrouted, the checkout at the end of it will
try to route again.
