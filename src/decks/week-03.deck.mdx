# The drift is not a manufacturing defect.

---

## The trolley as prosthesis

- a wheeled extension of the shopper's body, not a container for their goods
- built with just enough failure in it to keep that body moving past
  shelves it hadn't planned to see
- the basket-to-cart escalation runs the same logic, scaled up

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Containment dressed as convenience

A trolley that tracked perfectly straight would let you leave as directly
as you arrived. That's exactly what it's built not to do.

---

## The moment it stops being a container

Wheel geometry decides where a cart stops carrying your goods and starts
leashing your body through aisles it didn't choose.
