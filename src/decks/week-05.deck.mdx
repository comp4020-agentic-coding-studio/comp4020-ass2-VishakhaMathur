# The endcap spends momentum it didn't earn.

---

## Borrowed momentum

- the endcap takes the speed you were already carrying down the aisle
- and spends it on a decision you hadn't planned to make
- that's the whole trick, and it works whether you notice it or not

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Any elements, no matter where they are taken from, can serve in making
> new combinations.
>
> — Guy Debord and Gil J Wolman, *A User's Guide to Détournement*

---

## Détournement, worked

- **definition** — turning an existing sign against its own intention
- **method** — take one endcap apart, then rebuild its message backwards
- **not vandalism** — a counter-sign, aimed at the same shoppers the
  original was aimed at
