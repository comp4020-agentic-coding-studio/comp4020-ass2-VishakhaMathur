# The Job the Machine Didn't Keep

Lecture 8 — SLOP2236

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# It didn't remove a job. It handed the job to you, unpaid.

---

## De-skilling, run backwards

- a machine usually absorbs a worker's task
- self-checkout exports the task back to the customer instead, and keeps
  the wage
- calling the arrangement convenient is the whole trick

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## "Unexpected item in the bagging area"

Not a glitch line. The machine disciplining you, in real time, for doing
its job wrong.

---

## A small, constant surveillance apparatus

Weight sensors, cameras, a screen that watches you scan — and one moment,
still, where it needs a human to intervene.

---

## Before next week

- use a self-checkout, if one is nearby
- notice every instruction it gives you, and the exact words it uses
- name the one moment it had to ask a human for help
