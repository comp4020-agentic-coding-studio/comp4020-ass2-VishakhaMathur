# One receipt. Eleven mechanisms it doesn't itemise.

---

## The eleven, in one pass

the route, the trolley, the shelf, the endcap, the wall, the queue, the
checkout, the music, the card, the car park — and the door that opened all
of it in week one.

---

## What a receipt records

- price, quantity, tax, a loyalty number
- not the route it made you walk, not the wait it made you stand, not the
  music that set your pace
- the only document the store gives you back, and it accounts for none of
  the mechanism

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## State the argument. No hedging.

The supermarket is not a place of commerce. It's a mechanism, built to
route bodies and extract capital while calling the whole arrangement
service.
