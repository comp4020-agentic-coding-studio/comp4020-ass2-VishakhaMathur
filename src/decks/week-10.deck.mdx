# The discount is the fee you pay to be tracked.

## The reward that replaces the relation

- the card's "reward" simulates gratitude while performing surveillance
- the two are no longer distinguishable to the shopper holding it
- a very good deal — for the store

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> The simulacrum is never that which conceals the truth — it is the truth
> which conceals that there is none.
>
> — Jean Baudrillard, *Simulacra and Simulation*
