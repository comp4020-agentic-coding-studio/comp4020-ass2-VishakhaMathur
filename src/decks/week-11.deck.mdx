# Nowhere, Exactly Repeated

Lecture 11 — SLOP2236

# It performs no relationship with you at all.

---

## The non-place, almost too exactly

- no identity, no relations, no history
- less of a relationship than the store performs at its own threshold
- identical, branch to branch, chain to chain — and that repetition is the
  point

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> If a place can be defined as relational, historical and concerned with
> identity, then a space which cannot be defined as relational, or
> historical, or concerned with identity will be a non-place.
>
> — Marc Augé, *Non-Places*

---

## Pure threshold

Nobody photographs a car park unless something is wrong. That's what a
non-place is: unremarkable by design, everywhere at once.
