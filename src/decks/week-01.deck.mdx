# The door did not open for you.

---

## It opened for a sensor

A body crossed a beam. The door moved because the beam said move, not
because anyone behind it was glad to see you.

- no door in this building opens on welcome alone
- "welcome" is the sound the mechanism makes while it works

---

## The strategy and the tactic

De Certeau's split, and where it starts:

- **strategy** — the door, the beam, the building it was designed into
- **tactic** — what your body does with a threshold it didn't design
- everything from week two onward is this same split, aimed lower

---

## This week's method

Find the mechanism before you judge it. That's the whole course.
