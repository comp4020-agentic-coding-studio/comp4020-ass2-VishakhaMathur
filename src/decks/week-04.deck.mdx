# List everything. Then ask what the list means.

---

## The infra-ordinary

- Perec's method: exhaustive description before interpretation
- describe what's actually there, not what you expect to see
- applied to a shelf, "neutral" doesn't survive the first count

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Observe the street, from time to time, with some concern for system
> perhaps.
>
> — Georges Perec, *Species of Spaces*

---

## Eye level is a market, not a fact of anatomy

- shelf space at eye height is rented, priced, and sold
- the price tells you exactly whose attention the store has decided is
  worth buying — an adult's, a child's, a brand's placement fee
- nothing here is subtle once you've counted it
