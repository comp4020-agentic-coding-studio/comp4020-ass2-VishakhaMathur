## Before the session

Bring a shopping list of three items you actually need this week. Nothing
else.

## In the session

We trace the store's real floor plan against its printed one. The aisle
order is a strategy in de Certeau's sense — designed from above, indifferent
to any one shopper — and your habitual route through it is a tactic, a way of
using a space you did not design. The gap between the two is the whole
discipline of retail geography: milk at the back is not poor planning, it is
the plan.

## Afterwards

Keep the traced route. In week six you will need it again, once the argument
has more evidence behind it.
