## Before the session

Notice the music on your next visit, deliberately, for once. Note where in
the store it changed.

## In the session

Retail sound design sets a walking pace the way a metronome sets a tempo,
and it varies by zone — slower near high-margin fresh goods, quicker near
the checkout so the queue doesn't feel as long as it is. This is the sonic
floor: an ambient instruction you comply with before you've registered it as
an instruction at all.

## Afterwards

Everything so far has worked on your body without your consent. Week ten is
the first mechanism that asks for it directly, and gets it.
