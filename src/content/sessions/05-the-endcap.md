## Before the session

On your next store visit, photograph one endcap. Do not buy from it yet.

## In the session

Détournement — turning an existing sign against its own intention — is a
situationist tool built for exactly this kind of manufactured urgency. The
endcap borrows the aisle's momentum and spends it on a decision you hadn't
planned to make. We take one apart in class, then talk about what détourning
it back would look like: not vandalism, a counter-sign.

## Afterwards

Keep the photograph. The final project asks for more of these than you think
you'll need.
