## Before the session

Find your own loyalty card, or a supermarket's sign-up form, and read what
it actually asks for.

## In the session

Baudrillard's simulacrum is useful here: the "reward" simulates gratitude
while performing surveillance, and the two have become indistinguishable to
the shopper holding the card. A discount is the fee the store pays you to
consent to being tracked, and it is a very good deal — for the store. We map
what a full year of one shopper's loyalty data actually describes about
them.

## Afterwards

The card follows you to the car, which is where week eleven picks the
argument back up.
