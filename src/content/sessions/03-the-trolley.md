## Before the session

Push an empty trolley in a straight line for ten metres. Note what your arms
do.

## In the session

The trolley is the course's first prosthesis: a wheeled extension of the
shopper's body, built with just enough failure in it to keep that body
moving past shelves it hadn't planned to see. This is containment dressed as
convenience. We look at the wheel geometry, the basket-to-cart escalation,
and the moment a trolley stops being a container and starts being a leash.

## Afterwards

The next four weeks stay inside the store's engineered failures — the
trolley is the first, not the worst.
