## Before the session

Walk to a supermarket. Do not go in yet. Stand at the door and watch it work
on three strangers before it works on you.

## In the session

We take the door apart. Not literally — mechanically, in the sense the case
for the whole course rests on: a threshold engineered to look like
hospitality while doing the work of triage. Debord called the spectacle a
social relation mediated by images; the automatic door is the first image,
and it is already lying to you. De Certeau's distinction between the strategy
that built the door and the tactic of the body that crosses it starts here,
in week one, before you have bought anything.

## Afterwards

You leave with a method, not a verdict: notice the mechanism before you judge
it. Everything after week one is this same move, aimed at a different part of
the store.
