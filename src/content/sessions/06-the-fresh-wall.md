## Before the session

Recall the last store you were in. Try to draw its outer wall from memory
before you draw anything else.

## In the session

The "fresh wall" — dairy, produce, bakery, meat, run around the building's
edge — forces a lap of the whole floor to reach any one of them. This is
week two's route argument, scaled up to the size of the whole building: the
perimeter is not a shortcut, it is the longest reasonable path dressed as the
natural one.

## Afterwards

This is the week the dérive report gets set. Read the brief before next
session; you'll want the whole semester's inventory behind you when you
write it.
