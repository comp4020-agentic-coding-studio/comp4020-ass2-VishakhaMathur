## Before the session

Stand in one aisle. Without moving your head, list everything at eye level.
Then look down, and do it again.

## In the session

Perec's method — the exhaustive inventory of what is actually there, before
any of it is interpreted — is how this week works. Eye-level shelf space is
rented, not given, and its price tells you exactly whose attention the store
considers valuable: an adult's, a child's pulling at a sleeve, a brand's
whose placement fee bought that shelf specifically. Nothing here is subtle
once you have counted it.

## Afterwards

Bring your inventory to week five; the endcap is what happens when this same
logic stops pretending to be subtle.
