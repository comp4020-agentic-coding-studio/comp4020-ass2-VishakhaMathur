## Before the session

Next time you're in one, spend thirty seconds actually looking at a
supermarket car park instead of walking through it.

## In the session

Augé's non-place — a space of transit with no identity, no relations, no
history — describes the car park exactly, and describes it better than it
describes the store itself, which at least performs a relationship with you
at the door. The car park performs nothing. It is pure threshold, repeated
identically outside every branch of every chain, and that repetition is the
point.

## Afterwards

The car park is where the argument leaves the building. Week twelve is where
it has to add up to something.
