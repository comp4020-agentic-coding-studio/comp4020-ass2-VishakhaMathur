## Before the session

Use a self-checkout, if one is nearby, and notice every instruction it gives
you.

## In the session

"Unexpected item in the bagging area" is not a glitch line, it's the machine
disciplining you in real time for doing its job wrong. Self-checkout is
de-skilling running in reverse — normally a machine absorbs a worker's task,
here it exports the task back to the customer and keeps the wage. We read
the checkout as a small, constant surveillance apparatus: weight sensors,
cameras, a screen that watches you scan.

## Afterwards

The machine that watches you at the checkout was setting your pace long
before you reached it. Week nine is where that pace gets set.
