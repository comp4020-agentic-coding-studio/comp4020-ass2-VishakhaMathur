## Before the session

Queue somewhere this week — any queue — and time it.

## In the session

The queue is not a delay. It is the supermarket's confession: that you were
never a customer, only a body waiting to be processed. Stand in it. Time it.
Notice what they make you look at while you wait — the last unscripted
moment in the building, and it will not last. We compare notes: whose queue
had a magazine rack, whose had a card reader begging for round-up, whose had
another human being who talked to them.

## Afterwards

The queue ends at a machine, in most stores now. Week eight is that machine.
