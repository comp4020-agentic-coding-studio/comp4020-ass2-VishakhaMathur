## Before the session

Keep a receipt from this week. Read it properly, for once, before you throw
it out.

## In the session

We close the loop the door opened in week one. Eleven mechanisms — the
route, the trolley, the shelf, the endcap, the perimeter, the queue, the
checkout, the music, the card, the car park — and one receipt that accounts
for none of them. This is the last session before the counter-mapping
project, and it asks you to state, plainly, what the semester has actually
argued.

## Afterwards

The final project is due at the end of the teaching period. Bring the whole
semester's inventory; you will need all of it.
