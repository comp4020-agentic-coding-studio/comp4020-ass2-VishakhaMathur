## The brief

> Make the map the store would not make of itself.

A counter-map is not a critique bolted onto a floor plan. It reroutes the
building: relabel the fresh wall as a perimeter tax, redraw the queue as the
only unclaimed territory in the store, mark the endcaps as checkpoints. The
form is yours — a redrawn plan, an annotated photo series, a fictional
store guide, a rewritten receipt — but it has to reroute something real, not
just comment on it.

Twelve weeks gave you twelve mechanisms. Four is the floor, not the target;
use as many as the map can carry without becoming a list.

## What you submit

The counter-map itself, in whatever format carries it, plus a one-page
key naming which mechanism each part of the map reroutes and from which
week it came. A marker who has read your dérive report should recognise the
same store; a marker who hasn't should still be able to read the map cold.
