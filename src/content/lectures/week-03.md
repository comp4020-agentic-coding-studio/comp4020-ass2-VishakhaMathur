A trolley that tracked perfectly straight would let you leave the store as
directly as you arrived. It doesn't, and the drift isn't a manufacturing
defect — it's engineered friction, just enough to keep a body moving past
shelves it hadn't planned to see.

## Outline

- the trolley as prosthesis: an extension of the shopper's body, not a
  container for their goods
- wheel geometry and the basket-to-cart escalation
- containment dressed as convenience
