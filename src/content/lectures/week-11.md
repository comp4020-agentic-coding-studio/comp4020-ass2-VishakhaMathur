The car park has no identity, no relations, no history — Augé's non-place,
almost too exactly. It performs less of a relationship with you than the
store itself does at the threshold, and its identical repetition outside
every branch of every chain is precisely the point.

## Outline

- non-place, defined against the store's own performed welcome
- what repeats, chain to chain, without variation
- the car park as pure threshold
