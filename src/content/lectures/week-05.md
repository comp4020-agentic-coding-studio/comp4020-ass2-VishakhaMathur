The endcap borrows momentum you were already carrying down the aisle and
spends it on a decision you hadn't planned to make. Détournement — turning a
sign against its own intention — is the situationist tool built for exactly
this, and it's ours for the rest of the semester.

## Outline

- the endcap as inherited momentum, not a fresh pitch
- détournement: definition, and one worked example
- what a counter-sign looks like, as distinct from vandalism
