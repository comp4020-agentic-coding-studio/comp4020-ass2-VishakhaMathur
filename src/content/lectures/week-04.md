Perec's method is exhaustive description before interpretation: list
everything, then ask what the list means. Applied to a shelf, it stops
being neutral fast — eye-level space is rented at a price, and the price
tells you exactly whose attention the store has decided is worth buying.

## Outline

- the infra-ordinary: describing what's actually there, not what you expect
  to see
- eye level as a market, not a fact of anatomy
- who a shelf is actually built to catch
