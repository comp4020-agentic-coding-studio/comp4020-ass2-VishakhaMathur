Ambient music sets a walking pace the way a metronome sets a tempo, and it
changes by zone: slower near high-margin fresh goods, quicker near the
checkout so the queue feels shorter than it is. You comply with the
instruction before you register it as one.

## Outline

- tempo as an instruction, not a mood
- how the pace changes across a single store's zones
- what a store with no ambient sound would have to do instead
