A machine usually absorbs a worker's task. Self-checkout exports the task
back to the customer instead, keeps the wage, and calls the arrangement
convenient. "Unexpected item in the bagging area" is the machine disciplining
you for doing its job wrong.

## Outline

- de-skilling run backwards: the task returns to the customer, unpaid
- weight sensors, cameras, the screen that watches you scan
- the one moment the machine still needs a human
