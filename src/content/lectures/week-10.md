The card's "reward" simulates gratitude while performing surveillance, and
the two are no longer distinguishable to the shopper holding it. A discount
is the price the store pays you to consent to being tracked — a very good
deal, for the store.

## Outline

- simulacrum: the reward that replaces the relation it imitates
- what a loyalty sign-up form actually asks for, versus what it needs
- mapping a year of one shopper's data
