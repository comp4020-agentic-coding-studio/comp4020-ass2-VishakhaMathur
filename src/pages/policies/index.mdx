# Policies and support

## Late work

Work submitted after its deadline loses 5% of the available mark per day,
to a maximum of five days, after which it is not accepted. This applies
equally to the dérive report and the counter-mapping project; there is no
separate allowance for fieldwork running long.

## Extensions

Extensions are arranged before the deadline, not after it. Email the
convenor with what's already done and what's left — a request made once the
deadline has passed is treated as a late submission, not an extension.

## Academic integrity

Cite every theorist you name, and every store you describe truthfully: an
invented supermarket is a legitimate choice, a real one misdescribed to fit
the argument is not. Any assistance you used in producing the work —
including this course's own agentic tools — is declared in the submission,
not hidden by it.

## Getting help

Ask in the session first; most questions are more useful answered where the
rest of the class can hear them. Outside sessions, email whichever of
[Marisol](/people/marisol-quaye/) or [Idris](/people/idris-fenn/) fits the
question — course-level to the convenor, work-in-progress to the tutor.
