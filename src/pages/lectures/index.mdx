The lecture names the mechanism before the session sends you out to find it.
Twelve weeks, twelve theory drops — Debord's spectacle, de Certeau's
strategies and tactics, Perec's inventory, Baudrillard's simulacrum, Augé's
non-place — each one handed over just before that week's fieldwork puts it
to use, never after.
